# AGENTS.md

## Issues

An issue's body is its current state. When the work moves on, edit the body
instead of adding a comment, in any femiwiki repository.

- Lead with what the issue is, then the outcome, or what remains and what
  blocks it.
- Keep measurements, decisions, traps and references. Drop superseded plans
  and corrections of earlier text.
- Comment only to answer a person. Once that exchange is settled, fold what it
  decided into the body.
- A body cannot exceed 65,536 characters. Move reference material to the issue
  it belongs to, such as a sub-issue, rather than leaving it in comments.
