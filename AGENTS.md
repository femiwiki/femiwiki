# AGENTS.md

## Issues

An issue's body is its current state. When the work moves on, including
progress, measurements and a change of plan, edit the body instead of adding a
comment, in any femiwiki repository.

- Comment only to answer a person. Once that exchange is settled, fold what it
  decided into the body.
- When a body runs out of room, move reference material to the issue it belongs
  to, such as a sub-issue, not to comments.
